# Accessible Workspaces

A practical design and operations checklist for making workplaces accessible as one connected journey: arriving, finding the work area, doing the work, using shared facilities, getting support, and leaving safely.

The checklist is intended for workplace designers, facilities and estates teams, employers, managers, access professionals, and disabled and neurodivergent workers reviewing a workspace together.

## What this repository provides

`Accessible_Workspaces_Design_Checklist.md` brings together prompts for reviewing accessibility across four parts of the workplace experience:

1. **Knowing where you are** — pre-arrival information, maps, signs, consistent room names, and sensory-environment guidance.
2. **The route to where you work** — continuous step-free routes, usable controls, accessible security and reception procedures, and route checks after layout changes.
3. **The work area and shared facilities** — adaptable workstations, display-screen assessments, lighting and acoustics, assistive technology, meeting rooms, kitchens, toilets, lockers, and recovery spaces.
4. **Support, emergency arrangements and review** — adjustment requests, accessible information, evacuation planning, end-to-end testing, and reassessment after change.

The checklist uses three perspectives:

- **P — Physical access**
- **N — Non-visual, hearing, or other sensory access**
- **C — Cognitive and neuroinclusive access**

A checked item should mean that the feature works in practice for the people and tasks involved—not only that it exists on paper.

## How to use the checklist

1. Open [`Accessible_Workspaces_Design_Checklist.md`](Accessible_Workspaces_Design_Checklist.md).
2. Review each section with the people who use or maintain the workspace, including disabled and neurodivergent workers where possible.
3. Mark an item complete only after observing or testing the real route, room, facility, technology, or process.
4. Record actions, owners, and review dates outside the checklist or in your organisation's improvement-tracking system.
5. Revisit the checklist after refurbishment, relocation, changes to furniture or room use, new booking or security systems, changes of role, or reports of a barrier.

The checklist is a practical review aid and does not replace an individual workplace assessment, professional advice, risk assessment, or applicable legal requirements.

## Scope and reference material

The guidance is written with workplace accessibility in England in mind and links to relevant reference material, including:

- [Approved Document M, Volume 2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/access-to-and-use-of-buildings-approved-document-m)
- [HSE: disabled workers and employer duties](https://www.hse.gov.uk/disability/employers-duties/index.htm)
- [HSE: display screen equipment assessment](https://www.hse.gov.uk/msd/dse/assessment.htm)
- [Acas: reasonable adjustments](https://www.acas.org.uk/reasonable-adjustments)
- [Acas: adjustments for neurodiversity](https://www.acas.org.uk/reasonable-adjustments/adjustments-for-neurodiversity)
- [BSI: PAS 6463 and design for the mind](https://www.bsigroup.com/en-GB/insights-and-media/insights/brochures/pas-6463-design-for-the-mind-neurodiversity-and-the-built-environment/)
- [RNIB: Building Sight](https://www.rnib.org.uk/living-with-sight-loss/independent-living/building-sight/)
- [GOV.UK: means of escape for disabled people](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fire-safety-risk-assessment-means-of-escape-for-disabled-people/fire-safety-risk-assessment-means-of-escape-for-disabled-people)

Always confirm the current requirements and guidance that apply to your location, building, organisation, and circumstances.

## Contributing

Suggestions that make the checklist clearer, more practical, inclusive, or better supported by evidence are welcome. When proposing a change:

- explain which part of the end-to-end work journey it improves;
- use clear, practical language that can be tested in a real workspace;
- preserve the P/N/C perspective labels where relevant; and
- include authoritative sources for new compliance or practice guidance.

Please open an issue or pull request with the proposed change and its rationale.
